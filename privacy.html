Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Recurra ("the app") is a subscription and recurring-bill tracker. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Data you enter

Subscription names, amounts, dates, categories and notes you type into the app are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to us. Deleting the app, or using Settings → Delete all data, removes them permanently. There is no account system, so we cannot see, recover or restore your data.

Reminders

Renewal reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No notification content leaves your device.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. The app requests non-personalized ads. To serve ads and prevent fraud, Google may process device information such as an advertising identifier, IP address and coarse location. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Purchasing Recurra Pro removes all ads.

Purchases

Recurra Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and restore them across devices. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user identifier and purchase receipts; it does not receive your name, email or the data you enter in the app. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No accounts, no email collection
  • No selling or sharing of personal data
  • No access to contacts, photos, location or microphone

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.
