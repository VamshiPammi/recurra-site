Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email support@ydebug.com and include your phone model and app version (shown at the bottom of Settings).

Frequently asked

Where is my data stored?
Only on your phone. There is no account and nothing is uploaded, so please back up your phone if you need to keep the data.

Why didn't I get a reminder?
Open Settings in the app and make sure Renewal reminders is on and the phone allows notifications for Rebilo. Battery-saver modes on some phones delay notifications.

I bought Pro on another phone.
Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the same Google Play account.

How do I delete everything?
Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling the app does the same.
